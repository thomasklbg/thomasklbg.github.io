  <p>I’m on the founding team at <a href="https://x.com/noeri_ai">Noeri</a>, the plural of intelligence. We’re working towards a future where no single monolithic model or system has to serve everyone’s needs, but you can create and own your AI that keeps learning and improving, with the size, strengths, and quirks that suit you.</p>

  <p>Previously, I was an <a href="https://ai.ethz.ch/">ETH AI Center</a> Postdoctoral Fellow, mentored by <a href="https://las.inf.ethz.ch/krausea">Andreas Krause</a> and <a href="https://gioramponi.github.io/">Giorgia Ramponi</a>. Before that, I worked with <a href="https://www.trinity.ox.ac.uk/people/marta-kwiatkowska" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Marta Kwiatkowska</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ljeA6CMAAAAJ" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Lukasz Szpruch</a> at <a href="https://www.turing.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Alan Turing Institute</a> and the <a href="https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">University of Oxford</a>. I completed my PhD at the <a href="https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">University of Oslo</a> in 2024, supervised by <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/christosdimitrakakis" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Christos Dimitrakakis</a>.</p>
